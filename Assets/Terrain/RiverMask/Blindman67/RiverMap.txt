Stacked height map extends the height map range from 0-255 for grey scale elevation map to 0-765 allowing for finer detail but still remain compatible with most image manipulation software. Be warned though that many image editing applications do not know it is a height map and converting to black and white will tend to compress the height at the top and bottom of the height range making middle gradients steeper.

To correctly convert the Stacked height map to elevations (which you may wish to do for you 3D game) 
For each pixel's colour channel

    int elevation = R + G + B;

For a normalised elevation 0-1

    float elevation = (R + G + B) / 765.0;
    
    
This map was auto generated from the hand drawn river map and there is a little noise at the edges (2pixels in on all sides) that you may wish to cut out.

Occlusion is my own algorithm and is not an real occlusion but rather a looks good approximation.
